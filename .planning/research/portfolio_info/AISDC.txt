Last week, I had the incredible opportunity to moderate a panel at AI Student developer conference (AISDC) 2025 🎤 — and what an experience it was!

The panel, titled “Your AI Journey Starts Here: Breaking In and Building Success – For Youths, By Youths,” centred on real, relatable stories around internships, research, and scholarships in AI. I was honoured to guide such a meaningful discussion, especially with a room full of aspiring young minds.

A massive thank you to our wonderful panellists Alysa Tan, Jewel Aw, and Zaer Z. 🙏 — your insights, energy, and experiences made this session a success, and I’m so grateful for the patience when working with you.

Being a first-time moderator at an event of this scale was intense. From crafting personalized questions to designing interactive audience moments (👋 quick-fire rounds and hand-raise polls!), it pushed me way outside my comfort zone — and I loved every second of it.
I feel that I could have definitely done better — especially in delivery — but showing up, learning by doing, and sharing that stage was more than worth it.
