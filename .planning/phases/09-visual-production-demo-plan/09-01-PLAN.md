**Document structure:**

## Overview
- 2 demo recordings needed: Cold Open (Beat 1, 5s) and Result (Beat 6, 6-8s)
- Target site: nim-kaleb.vercel.app
- Both require the WS server at ws.kalebnim.dev to be running and responsive

## Pre-Recording Setup
1. **Browser configuration:** Use Chrome or Arc, hide bookmarks bar, set window to 1920x1080 or 2560x1440 (specify both as options). Zoom level 100%. No extensions visible. Clear URL bar of suggestions.
2. **Site state:** Navigate to nim-kaleb.vercel.app in a fresh incognito window. Let the boot sequence complete. Wait for cursor to blink at command input (terminal in MENU state).
3. **Screen capture tool:** OBS Studio or macOS Screen Recording (Command+Shift+5). Record at native resolution. Include system audio (AI voice output). Do NOT include mic audio in screen recording (that comes from talking head recording separately).
4. **Audio routing:** Ensure AI voice output is routed to screen recording. On macOS, use BlackHole or Loopback to capture system audio. Test by recording 5 seconds and playing back -- AI voice must be audible in recording.
5. **Network check:** Confirm ws.kalebnim.dev is reachable: open browser console, check for WebSocket connection success after clicking "Activate Voice Interface."
6. **Fallback strategy:** If WS server is down or latency is too high on recording day, have a pre-recorded backup of each demo ready. Record backup demos at least 1 day before filming.

## Demo Recording 1: Cold Open (Beat 1)

**Purpose:** 5-second hook showing the site working -- visitor asks a question, AI responds in Kaleb's cloned voice.

**Setup steps (in order):**
1. Open nim-kaleb.vercel.app in incognito Chrome at 1920x1080
2. Let boot sequence complete (wait ~5 seconds for typewriter to finish)
3. Type "1" and press Enter to select "Activate Voice Interface"
4. Wait for "Connecting..." and then voice interface to be ready (waveform area visible)
5. Start screen recording
6. Type or speak: "Hey, who are you?" (per D-01)
7. Let AI respond for at least 5 seconds
8. Stop recording

**Capture requirements:**
- Duration: Record at least 10 seconds, trim to best 5 seconds in post
- Frame: Full browser window, terminal modal centered, starfield visible
- Audio: AI clone voice must be clearly audible, no background noise
- Visual details that must be visible: green monospace text (#00FF00) typing, phosphor glow effect, audio waveform indicator pulsing, command input with question text
- The AI should be mid-sentence at the 5-second mark (creates intrigue per storyboard)

**Takes:** Record 3-5 takes. AI responses vary each time; pick the take where:
  - Voice sounds most natural and conversational
  - Response content is interesting/engaging (not a generic greeting)
  - Text typing is clearly visible and readable

**Output:** Single video file, labeled `demo-cold-open-take-N.mov`

## Demo Recording 2: Result Demo (Beat 6)

**Purpose:** 6-8 second demo showing voice quality and conversational depth -- proving "it actually sounds like me."

**Setup steps (in order):**
1. Open nim-kaleb.vercel.app in a fresh incognito window (new session, NOT reusing the cold open session)
2. Complete boot sequence, activate voice interface
3. Start screen recording
4. Ask: "Tell me about a project you're proud of" (or similar open-ended question -- see alternatives below)
5. Let AI respond for at least 8 seconds
6. Stop recording

**Alternative questions (if primary doesn't produce good response):**
- "What's the hardest bug you've ever fixed?" (per Phase 8 decision)
- "What makes you different from other engineers?"
- "Tell me about something you built that surprised you"

**Capture requirements:**
- Duration: Record at least 15 seconds, trim to best 6-8 seconds in post
- Frame: Same as cold open -- full browser, terminal centered
- Audio: Voice quality is the focus -- must sound natural, conversational, with personality (not robotic)
- Key difference from cold open: This demo emphasizes voice naturalness; viewer has been primed by 50 seconds of story about voice cloning, so they're listening for voice quality
- The AI should be mid-answer showing depth (not a one-liner)

**Takes:** Record 5+ takes (more than cold open because voice quality selection is more subjective). Pick the take where:
  - Voice has the most natural cadence and intonation
  - Response shows depth and personality (not a surface-level answer)
  - No audio glitches, cuts, or unnatural pauses

**Output:** Single video file, labeled `demo-result-take-N.mov`

2. Clear browser state (new incognito window)
3. Record all result demo takes (can vary questions across takes)
4. Review takes immediately -- if none are good, re-record while setup is still active
