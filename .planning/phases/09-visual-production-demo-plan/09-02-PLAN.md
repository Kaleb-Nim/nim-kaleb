## Camera Setup
- Device: iPhone (rear camera) or dedicated camera
- Resolution: 1080x1920 (9:16 portrait for TikTok) for talking head; 1920x1080 (16:9 landscape) for screen demos
- Frame rate: 30fps
- Orientation: Portrait (vertical) for talking head, landscape for screen demos
- Stabilization: Tripod or phone mount at eye level (do NOT handheld -- talking head needs stable framing)
- Distance: ~3 feet from face for upper body framing

## Lighting
- Primary: Ring light or desk lamp positioned directly in front, slightly above eye level
- Fill: Window light from side if available, or second lamp
- Avoid: Overhead fluorescent (creates harsh shadows under eyes), backlight from windows (silhouette)
- Test: Record 5 seconds and check -- face should be evenly lit, no dark shadows on one side

## Audio
- Talking head: Use AirPods Pro, lapel mic, or phone mic placed close (within 2 feet)
- Test: Record 5 seconds and play back -- voice should be clear, no room echo, no background hum
- Quiet environment: Turn off fans, close windows, silence notifications

## Wardrobe
- Solid-colored shirt (no patterns, no logos, no stripes -- these cause moire on camera)
- Dark colors preferred (matches the dark terminal theme of the site)
- No jewelry that makes noise or catches light distractingly
